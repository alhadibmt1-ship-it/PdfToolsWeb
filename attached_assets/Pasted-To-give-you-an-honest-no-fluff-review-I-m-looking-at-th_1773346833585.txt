​The Bad: You are missing "Identity." Who is behind PDF HUB 24? Is it a company? An individual developer?
​Fix: You need an "About Us" page. Even a simple one explaining that you built this tool to provide a faster, ad-free experience for users in Pakistan and the US will make Google trust the domain much more.

​The Bad: The internal links in the footer and the "Related Tools" grid are a bit repetitive.
