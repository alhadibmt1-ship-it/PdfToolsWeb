# Content Briefs — Priority Gap Pages

Two briefs, based on real gaps found by comparing actual current page content
against the keyword clusters identified. Both are lower-competition
opportunities within already-high-volume clusters — the realistic near-term
wins, not head-term chasing.

---

## Brief 1: "PDF to DOC Converter" angle
**Target keyword:** `pdf to doc converter` (58K/mo, lower competition than
"pdf to word")
**Secondary terms to work in:** pdf to doc, .doc file converter, legacy word
format
**Target page:** `/pdf-to-word` (add a section — do not create a new
duplicate page; this would repeat the exact duplicate-content mistake this
whole project spent months fixing)

**The gap:** Current content on `/pdf-to-word` exclusively uses "DOCX"
phrasing (verified: zero mentions of ".doc" or "converter" phrasing in the
current copy). Some searchers specifically want the older `.doc` format
(older Word versions, some government/legal systems still require it) or
search with "converter" in the phrase rather than "convert."

**What to add** (a new H2 section, not a rewrite):
- Address the DOC vs. DOCX distinction directly: what's the difference, which
  one does this tool output, and — critically — **verify with engineering
  what format the tool actually outputs before writing this**. If it's
  DOCX-only, be upfront about that rather than imply .doc support that
  doesn't exist (this is exactly the kind of overclaim this project has
  repeatedly had to walk back — check first, then write).
- One FAQ entry addressing "Can I get a .doc file instead of .docx?"
- Natural mention of "converter" phrasing in a subheading (e.g., "Free PDF to
  Word Converter" alongside existing "PDF to Word" phrasing) — captures the
  distinct search phrasing without duplicating the page.

**Do NOT:** create a separate `/pdf-to-doc` page. That's a near-duplicate of
`/pdf-to-word` — the exact pattern already removed from 46 other pages this
project.

## Brief 2: "JPG Size Reducer" angle
**Target keyword:** `jpg size reducer` (87K/mo, meaningfully lower competition
than "image compressor" or "compress image")
**Secondary terms:** reduce jpg file size, shrink jpg, jpg compressor free
**Target page:** `/image-compressor` (add a section, same reasoning as above)

**The gap:** Current content on `/image-compressor` is framed generically
around "images" and "photos." The specific phrase "size reducer" (as opposed
to "compressor") suggests a distinct user intent — often someone hitting a
specific upload limit (a form, an application, a website) rather than general
photo optimization.

**What to add:**
- A section framed around **specific size targets** ("reduce JPG to under
  100KB," "shrink a photo for an online form") — mirrors the successful
  pattern already used on `/compress-pdf`'s long-tail size-target pages
  (`compress-pdf-to-1mb`, etc.), which are already your best-performing
  differentiated content pattern.
- One FAQ: "How do I reduce a JPG to a specific file size?"
- Natural use of "reduce" and "shrink" phrasing alongside existing "compress"
  language.

**Possible follow-up (bigger task, don't do yet):** if this angle performs,
consider 2-3 genuine long-tail pages under `/tools/` for specific common size
targets people search for with images (e.g., "reduce jpg to 200kb online") —
same proven pattern as the compress-pdf size variants, but only after
confirming real demand, not speculatively.

## Why these two and not the head terms
`pdf to word` (4.0M), `merge pdf` (1.7M), `compress pdf` (1.6M) are the
biggest volume terms in the niche, but they're also the most fought-over —
entrenched competitors with a decade of backlinks (pdf24.org, iLovePDF,
Smallpdf) occupy the top results. These two briefs target the **lower-KD
variants within those same clusters** — genuinely realistic to move on in
weeks/months rather than years.
