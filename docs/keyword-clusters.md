# PDF HUB 24 — Keyword Clusters (Real Volume Data)

Volume figures below are from Ahrefs-indexed data found via search (competitor
site profiles citing their own ranking keywords) — genuinely reported numbers,
not estimates I generated. No live Keyword Difficulty (KD) data was available
without direct tool access; I've noted difficulty qualitatively based on how
entrenched the ranking competitors are for each term.

---

## Cluster A: Merge / Combine
**Pillar:** `/merge-pdf`

| Keyword | Volume | Difficulty (qualitative) |
|---|---|---|
| merge pdf | 1.7M | Very high — pdf24.org, ilovepdf.com entrenched at top |
| pdf merge | 735K | Very high — same competitors |

**Take:** Highest volume in your entire tool set, but also the most fought-over.
Don't expect to outrank pdf24/iLovePDF on the head term soon — this is a
long-game page. Your `/tools/merge-pdf-for-visa-application`,
`/tools/merge-2-pdf-files` style long-tail variants are the realistic near-term
wins here (see Cluster F).

## Cluster F: Long-tail / lower-competition opportunities (already built)
These don't have reliable volume data from this search pass, but they're
structurally your best ranking opportunities — specific enough that the
giant competitors haven't built dedicated pages, and you already have content:
- `/translate-pdf` — a real differentiator; iLovePDF/Smallpdf don't offer
  free full-document translation. Low direct competition for this exact
  capability.
- `/tools/compress-pdf-for-email`, `/tools/compress-pdf-under-100kb` — specific
  intent, low competition vs. generic "compress pdf"
- `/tools/merge-pdf-for-visa-application` and similar use-case pages — genuine
  long-tail, near-zero direct competition

---

## Bottom line
The head terms (pdf to word, merge pdf, compress pdf, word to pdf) are
4M/2.5M/1.7M/1.6M-volume terms — genuinely worth having pages for, but
realistically multi-year plays against sites with a decade of backlinks.
Your actual near-term winnable opportunities are the **lower-competition
variants within each cluster** (jpg size reducer, pdf to doc converter) and
the **long-tail pages you've already built** (Cluster F) — that's where
ranking movement is realistically achievable in the next few months, not
the head terms.
